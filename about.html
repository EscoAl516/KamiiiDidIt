THE STYLIST

Kamia Tolley

I braid out of Garden City, one client in the chair at a time. That is on purpose. Nobody sits waiting while I finish someone else, and nobody leaves half-done because the day ran out.

Most of what I do is protective — knotless, box, twists, locs. If your edges are tender or you are growing hair back out, say so when you book and I will size the parts for it.
